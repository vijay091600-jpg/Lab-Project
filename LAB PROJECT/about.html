About NMSM Government College

Empowering Students Through Knowledge, Values and Innovation.

Our College

NMSM Government College, Kalpetta, is one of the leading government colleges in Wayanad. The college provides quality higher education with experienced faculty, modern facilities and a student-friendly campus.

Vision

To become a centre of excellence by providing quality education, promoting innovation, and developing socially responsible graduates.

Mission

  • Provide quality education.
  • Develop leadership and creativity.
  • Promote research and innovation.
  • Build responsible citizens.

Campus Facilities

  • Modern Library
  • Computer Labs
  • Science Laboratories
  • Sports & Gymnasium
  • Ladies Hostel
  • Green Campus
  • NCC & NSS Units
